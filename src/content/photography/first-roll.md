Everyone has a first roll. The one where you realise that photography isn't just taking pictures — it's about *seeing*.

These were shot on a grey January morning in Tel Aviv. The light was flat but the streets were full of texture: coffee cups, people rushing, cats doing what cats do. I almost deleted half of them. Glad I didn't.

More to come. Many more.
