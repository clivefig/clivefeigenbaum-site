A classic Speyside expression from one of the last independently-owned family distilleries in Scotland. Matured entirely in oloroso sherry casks — no blending, no tricks.

If you're looking for a sherry-forward whisky that won't break the bank, the Glenfarclas 12 is the place to start. Every sip tastes like someone lit a fire and put a Christmas pudding in front of you.

**Verdict:** A go-to bottle. Always one on the shelf.
